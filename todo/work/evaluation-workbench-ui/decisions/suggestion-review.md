## Question

How should generated tags, observations, bundle boundaries, criteria, and related
suggestions enter human review at useful speed without acquiring the authority of
approved records?

Prototype individual and batch review, including accept, edit, reject, defer,
source expansion, conflict with existing human work, partial coverage, low or
unavailable confidence, producer revisions, and measured samples of unflagged
content. Decide which suggestion types may share a queue, how review progress and
coverage remain visible, and where bounded model-work scope, disclosure, cost,
and skipped material are inspected.
